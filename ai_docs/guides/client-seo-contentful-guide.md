## 1. How Your Website's Automatic SEO Works ("Set It & Forget It")

When you add or edit a **Stillage Product** or **Blog Post** in Contentful, **you do not need to write any code or worry about breaking Google rankings**.

### If You Leave the Optional SEO Fields Blank:
Our system automatically builds everything Google and AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) need from your standard product fields:
1. **Google Search Title:** Automatically combines your Product Title, SKU, and Safe Working Load (e.g. *3-Bay Double-Sided Heavy Duty Cantilever Bar & Pipe Stillage Rack (SSS-CANT-3B8A) — 1500kg SWL*).
2. **Google Search Description:** Automatically summarises your Short Description, External Dimensions (`mm`), Stacking Rating, and starting Ex. VAT price within Google's 160-character limit.
3. **Branded Social Share Card:** Automatically generates a 1200×630 high-contrast *British Engineered Steel* image card (`/api/og`) whenever a link is shared on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or email.
4. **Google Rich Product & FAQ Snippets:** Automatically publishes your Ex. VAT price, stock status, dimensions, SWL rating, and 4 technical FAQs directly into Google's structured data format (`JSON-LD`).
5. **Sitemap & AI Search Feed:** Automatically updates `/sitemap.xml` and `/llms.txt` so both Google and AI search assistants can recommend your stillages.

## 2. Optional: How to Customize SEO or FAQs in Contentful

If you want to customize how a specific product or guide appears on Google, open the entry in Contentful and scroll to the bottom three optional fields:

1. **SEO Meta Title (Optional — Max 65 characters)**
   - *Tip:* Keep it punchy and include the main keyword UK buyers search for (e.g. `Heavy-Duty 1200x1000mm Solid Sheet Steel Stillage | 1000kg SWL`).
2. **SEO Meta Description (Optional — Max 165 characters)**
   - *Tip:* Write 1–2 plain-English sentences explaining what the stillage holds, its SWL rating, and that you offer fast UK pallet delivery.
3. **Buyer FAQs for Google & AI Search (Optional)**
   - *How to format:* Simply type each question and answer on a single line separated by a vertical bar `|`:
     `Can this stillage be hot-dip galvanised for outdoor yards? | Yes, select Hot-Dip Galvanised (BS EN ISO 1461) in the online configurator for +£45 per unit.`

## 3. How to View & Share Your Website Analytics

You never need to log into complicated Google Analytics 4 (GA4) menus. We have set up three simple ways to see how your website is performing:

### Option A: Automated Monthly PDF Email (Recommended for Directors)
- **When:** Arrives automatically in your email inbox at **08:00 AM on the 1st of every month**.
- **What's inside:** A single, easy-to-read PDF page generated by Looker Studio showing:
  1. **Total UK Trade Visitors** (and % growth vs. last month)
  2. **Proforma Invoices & Online Orders Submitted**
  3. **Trade Desk Phone (`01978 800 500`) & Email Clicks**
  4. **Top 5 Most Viewed & Configured Stillages**
  5. **Top Search Phrases UK Buyers Typed Into Google & AI**
  6. **Top UK Postcode Regions Ordering Stillages**

### Option B: Live On-Site Executive Dashboard (`/analytics-report`)
- Visit **`https://stillagestoragesolutions.co.uk/analytics-report`** at any time (or click **"Client SEO, GEO & Monthly Analytics Dashboard"** in the website footer).
- View your 1-page Executive Scorecard, test live conversion events, and inspect the Google/AI SEO status of every Contentful product and technical guide.

### Option C: Optional Lightweight Mobile Dashboard (Plausible / Netlify Analytics)
- For instant, cookie-free daily visitor checks on your phone without any passwords or GDPR cookie banners, set `NEXT_PUBLIC_PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN=stillagestoragesolutions.co.uk` in Netlify environment variables.
